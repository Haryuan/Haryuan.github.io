Two Sides

In some situations, people with autism disorder are the same as others, but when they face social scenarios, difficulties come along. They may feel difficult to understand verbal and written language. Facing both language and social discrimination can cause significant psychological harm to them and exacerbate their isolation.

The typographic-led designs in this set are meant to represent the social inequality between normal people and autisms. The positive and negative spaces of the text form two words in opposite directions. One side visualizes the general experience of normal people, and the other side presents the difficulties experienced by those with autism during social interactions.
