The Gift

It's a gift from a 14-year-old to an 18-year-old. I just turned 18 this year, and in this symbolic point of adulthood, I am going through all kinds of life events, applications, exams, and defencs. The question "Who am I?" keeps recurring in my mind... Eventually, I decided to transform my first guitar when I was 14 and give it to myself as my adult gift.
