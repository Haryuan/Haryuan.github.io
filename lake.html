Lake of Xi

In this type design project, I created a font for my hometown, Hangzhou, titled "LAKE OF XI." The spirit of this typeface draws directly from the textures of West Lake itself.

It aims to capture nature in West Lake's purest form-especially the shifting, supple, and gentle movements of the water's surface. At the same time, it bears the weight of more than two thousand years of culture shaped around the lake. What it conveys is a quiet sense of calm and gravity-something that holds deep meaning for the city and for those who call it home.
