Urban Blocks

In the year following the end of the pandemic, I visited Wulianxiyuan, a urban village in Hangzhou. I was drawn to the migrant workers living in the urban village. During my multiple on-site inspections, I listened to the stories of people of different ages and professions.

In the end, I documented my observations and experiences, combining the visual language of the urban village to create a book. In addition, I introduced the concept of the "City in a Box," using various methods to convey the trapped lives of the urban village residents.
